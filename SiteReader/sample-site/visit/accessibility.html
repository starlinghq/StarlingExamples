Accessibility

The Central Library has step-free access from Quay Street, a lift to all floors, an accessible toilet on the ground floor and a hearing loop at the main desk.

The Harbour Branch is on the first floor and has no lift. Staff will bring requested items down to the harbour master's office on request; call ahead.

Large-print books, audiobooks and a magnifier reading station are available at the Central Library.

Alder Point Public Library, 14 Quay Street, Alder Point. Contact Staff area
