Branches

Central Library

14 Quay Street. The largest collection, the maker space, two meeting rooms and the local history archive. Bus routes 4 and 11 stop outside.

Harbour Branch

2 Ferry Lane, above the harbour master's office. A small lending collection, a children's corner and public computers.

Mobile library

The mobile library van visits Kelby, Marsh End and Upton Cross every second Wednesday. The timetable is posted at each stop.
