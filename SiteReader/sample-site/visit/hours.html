Opening hours

Central Library

The Central Library on Quay Street is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 19:00 and Saturday from 10:00 to 16:00. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Harbour Branch

The Harbour Branch is open Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday from 10:00 to 14:00.

Holiday closures

All branches close from 24 December to 1 January. The book drop at the Central Library stays open and returns are not charged late fees during the closure.
