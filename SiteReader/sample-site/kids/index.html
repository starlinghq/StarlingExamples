Children and teens

Rhyme Time for babies and toddlers runs every Monday and Friday at 10:30 at the Central Library. Story hour for ages 4 to 7 is on Saturdays at 11:00.

The teen code club meets on Wednesdays from 16:00 to 17:30 in the maker space and is free for ages 11 to 16. Homework help is available on weekday afternoons.

The summer reading challenge runs from July to September.

Alder Point Public Library, 14 Quay Street, Alder Point. Contact Staff area
