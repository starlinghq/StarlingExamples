Maker space

The maker space at the Central Library has two 3D printers, a laser cutter, sewing machines and a soldering bench. Book a one-hour slot at the desk; the laser cutter requires a 30-minute induction.

3D printing costs 10p per gram of filament. Volunteers from the Alder Point Makers group staff the space on Thursday evenings.

Alder Point Public Library, 14 Quay Street, Alder Point. Contact Staff area
