Tool library

Members aged 18 or over can borrow tools for up to one week: drills, ladders, sanders, a pressure washer, a tile cutter, gardening tools and camping gear.

Tools are collected from and returned to the Central Library maker space desk. A refundable deposit of £20 applies to power tools. Safety induction is required for the tile cutter.

Alder Point Public Library, 14 Quay Street, Alder Point. Contact Staff area
