Membership

Membership is free for anyone who lives, works or studies in the Alder Point district. Bring a photo ID and proof of address to any branch, or start online and finish at the desk.

Members can borrow up to 15 items for three weeks and renew twice unless another member has reserved the item. There are no late fees on children's books.
