Repair Café

Bring broken lamps, toasters, clothes and bikes to the Repair Café on the first Saturday of each month from 10:00 to 13:00 in the Estuary Room. Volunteer fixers show you how to repair them.

The Repair Café is run with the Alder Point Makers group. It is free; donations go towards replacement parts.

Alder Point Public Library, 14 Quay Street, Alder Point. Contact Staff area
